# Native Pet Town Battle Mode

Battle Mode is an optional solo prototype. Reopen the development desktop town to load the new native scripts, then press **Option + B** in ordinary town play to open the challenge screen. It also works in standalone Godot without live coding agents. Pet Street and public browser play keep their current behavior.

## Playing

Maple protects a fixed starting group of Nimbaa, Fiddlekit and Tuftlet in the existing village clearing. Move with WASD, run with Shift and jump with Space. There is no battle-only fence or invisible arena wall: Maple can roam, jump and swim through the existing town. Ordinary scenery, terrain and the finite world's outer edge still apply; this is not infinite terrain. Wildlife remains near its starting homes, so roaming away leaves it exposed. Orbit and zoom use the ordinary chase camera. Maple aims and fires green seeds automatically at the nearest visible skeleton within 14 units. Visible solid scenery blocks shots. Building, travel, gliding, pet switching and ordinary companion panels are unavailable during the match.

Choose **1, 3, 5 or 10 minutes** on the challenge screen (five is the default). After a three-second countdown, survive that many active minutes. Play again keeps the selected duration. Minion, Warrior, Rogue and Mage are introduced in that order in every duration, then mixed with common Minions and bounded special-role counts. Quiet cream name badges and distinct green stamps identify the existing models. Minion steadily chases and strikes. Warrior is slower and tougher, warning before a heavier melee strike and recovery. Rogue is fragile, warns a fixed short corridor, rushes without steering, and rests after hitting a living body, cover or its distance/time limit. Mage warns a locked position, then fires one straight non-homing shot; solid scenery blocks it and a living body can intercept it. Every role chooses the nearest reachable living Maple or wildlife, rather than favoring a different species. The amber ring/exclamation and committed corridor/shot line mean attacks, not spawning, and clear on recovery. Maple's launcher still deals 20 damage every 0.6 seconds. Spawn attempts gradually reach a sustained 1.6-second spacing during the first 45% of the selected duration, with at most sixteen living enemies and no final acceleration or new last-two-second spawn. Spawns sample nearby connected dry ground across the town, falling back to living wildlife when Maple is unreachable, such as while swimming.

Guidance is entirely passive. A hidden living animal—offscreen, behind the camera or occluded by solid scenery—gets its name, camera-relative bearing, approximate distance from Maple and battle-health percentage. A clearly visible animal loses duplicate navigation but retains useful health feedback and honestly labeled nearby-enemy caution. Critical health has priority over recent hit, then proximity caution, then other living animals; ties use lower health and then nearer distance. Same-bearing animals keep full named rows. Healthy guidance stays quiet. Out gets a brief neutral acknowledgement, then only summary/visible Out status, never rescue urgency or revival. Bearings are not a safe-route or travel-time promise. The bottom instruction strip is removed, not movement or automatic shooting.

Only one special/recovery/shot competes at a time. Hidden specials get an attack-specific amber role/action/place/bearing/fixed-aim notice, separate from proximity caution: below the threatened animal's desktop guide, or in the selected compact lower-right pocket above the wildlife summary. A special waits when a safe warning cannot fit. Losing visibility during preparation starts a full hidden warning; losing communication cancels unsafe active damage rather than silently delivering it. Initial numerical tuning is not a user-approved balance guarantee: Minion: 40 health/10 damage/0.6-second preparation/0.9 recovery; Warrior: 80/16/1.05/1.25; Rogue: 26/10/1.1/1.35; Mage: 30/8/1.2/1.7. Hidden preparation is at least 3.5 active seconds. Critical means 25% or less, recent-hit emphasis lasts 1.6 active seconds, nearby-enemy caution checks six units, and Out acknowledgement lasts two active seconds. No role gains inflated stats or faster attacks later in the run.

Maple and the three participating animals start at 100 battle health. There is one life and no healing. Wildlife wanders or flees on supported dry-ground paths; an animal at zero rests out of the match and contributes zero health. Losing wildlife does not end the run. Maple at zero ends it. Battle damage never enters wildlife care, friendship, world edits or coin records.

**Escape** or Pause freezes combat, projectiles, wildlife battle behavior, cooldowns, animation and active time. Switching away pauses automatically. Choose **Resume battle** deliberately. Leave requires confirmation and restores town. A battle temporarily uses a minimum logical window size of 480×520; the ordinary minimum returns afterward.

## Score and history

Completed score is `floor(10 × kills + 3 × Maple health% + 2 × average wildlife health% + 500)`. Defeat gives kill points only. Abandonment records an outcome with no score. Only completed runs contribute to the personal best. Bests compare the same duration, arena and rules; a one-minute score never replaces a ten-minute best. History labels each run's selected duration. Earlier five-minute records remain readable with their original best keys and rules. Scores are local points, independent of existing usage coins.

Results show the breakdown, save status, Play again, Return to town and History. Save failure retains the visible result with **Retry save** and does not claim a saved best. Returning restores the original actor/camera, companion presentation, wildlife and ordinary controls. External coding agents continue working; their bridge snapshots are buffered during the match and reconciled on return.

`user://battle-results.json` holds the latest twenty outcomes and versioned completed bests. Match IDs make repeated saves idempotent. Writes flush a temporary file before atomic replacement. Unreadable records are preserved with an `.unreadable-<timestamp>` suffix; if preserving the original fails, writes remain blocked. History explains the recovery issue. Native and browser save directories remain separate.

The current arena version is `open-town-v2`, with `solo-v3` rules. Prior `solo-v1`/`solo-v2` history and best records remain readable and retain their original keys; changed-role scores do not replace old-rules bests. The starting wildlife and Maple positions remain in the village clearing, but navigation samples dry ground across the full existing world rather than imposing a 30-unit radius or fixed elevation band. Enemy paths still require traversable terrain and clearance; Maple's movement is not restricted to that graph. Preparation validates fixed start/animal positions. Unsafe edits disable Start with an explanation; the mode never repairs or rearranges the town. Preparation yields across frames and can be cancelled with Back or Escape. Navigation is cached against committed world collision revisions, while fixed start and animal positions are rechecked before every match. Already queued terrain changes must finish before entry. Temporary animals remain frozen during cast preparation. No battle containment bodies are created. Maple retains ordinary collision and fall-through recovery without snapping back for a high jump, a swim or leaving the old clearing. All ordinary body layers and save keys remain compatible.

## Ownership and assets

- `scripts/battle/session.gd`: authoritative lifecycle, pause, finalization and input restrictions.
- `arena.gd`: world-wide supported dry-ground paths and local fair spawning. The former `boundary.gd` prototype is no longer instantiated.
- `context.gd`: reversible town/camera/body/care/bridge isolation.
- `combat.gd`: ordered role introduction, bounded mixtures/special competition, warning permission, projectile lifetime and freeze.
- `avatar.gd`, `enemy.gd`, `enemy_attack.gd`, `wildlife.gd`, `weapon.gd`, `enemy_shot.gd`: shared force-driven physics, explicit committed attack phases, isolated transient health and native swept projectiles. The enemy attack base keeps phases and warning state on the same physical body; the enemy script owns model setup, pursuit and defeat.
- `ui/battle/awareness.gd`: passive visibility/bearing/grouping and shared warning availability. `awareness_widgets.gd` owns wildlife widget presentation, and `role_badges.gd` renders role labels. None applies damage or changes controls.
- `store.gd`: isolated schema validation, atomic persistence, history and versioned bests.
- `ui/battle/`: selected corner HUD, full entry/result/history, centered pause and error/recovery states.
- `assets/battle/`: recovered licensed skeleton rigs and selected seed launcher, with asset provenance.

The three integration hooks are in native `sample.gd`, `town_input.gd` and `actor_motion.gd`. No pet-to-agent focus helper, native activation route, Rust bridge protocol or browser code changed.

The asset grip/aim and combat animation remain prototype work to assess in actual play.

## User-owned live acceptance

Build/import and an isolated short native smoke/render establish integration, not a complete or balanced game. Reopen through the development desktop's **Open Pet Town**, or use `sh apps/pet-town-godot-sample/tools/run.sh` for standalone play. An already installed release must be rebuilt/reinstalled to include changed resources.

1. Enter Battle Mode without an agent. Verify the full entry screen, three-second countdown, health/timer/kills/wildlife corners, automatic seeds, ordered Minion→Warrior→Rogue→Mage introductions and full-name/stamp identity. Observe a steady chase, warned heavy strike/recovery, fixed scenery-stopped rush/rest and dodgeable locked straight shot. Verify cover/interception, forgiving overlap and hidden-source/hidden-target warning or delay. Move/run/jump, orbit/zoom, shoot near cover, and check the muzzle and supporting arm while moving.
2. Protect wildlife and observe hits, brief hit protection, fleeing, knockout, enemy hit/fall/clear and one kill per defeated skeleton. Press R/J/K/H/V/F and building controls; none should escape the match. Walk beyond the former clearing rim in every direction, jump normally without snapping back, swim, and check terrain/roof collision. Verify nearby dry-ground enemies still spawn outside the clearing; when swimming, living wildlife must remain vulnerable.
3. Pause during a jump, a seed flight, a special windup, a Rogue rush and a Mage shot. Wait, resume, and verify the timer, positions, momentum, cooldowns and health continue together. Switch apps and return; the match must stay paused until Resume.
4. Select each of 1, 3, 5 and 10 minutes and verify the start label, countdown, timer and selected-duration pacing. Complete runs with different durations, then separately lose Maple's life. Verify best scores stay separated by duration and older five-minute history remains readable. Check completed/defeated breakdowns and no damage after results. Play again and verify the same chosen duration with fresh health, enemies, timer and match ID. Leave a paused match and confirm its history has no score or best update.
5. Begin while following a companion, leave after more than fifteen seconds, and check the same still-live companion/camera returns. End that agent or disconnect/reconnect during a run; Maple must remain independent, and return must reconcile the current roster safely. Confirm care and world saves did not acquire combat changes.
6. Reopen the app and check latest history and completed best. For a user-owned storage failure exercise, back up the isolated battle file. With the app closed, confirm there is no existing `battle-results.json.tmp`, then create an empty directory with that exact name beside the battle file to block only its temporary write. Finish a run, remove that empty directory, and choose Retry save. Verify one record and no false saved-best status. Never change world/care records or their directory permissions for this check.
7. Move wildlife offscreen, behind the camera and behind solid scenery. Verify identity/bearing/approximate distance/health, critical/recent-hit/nearby-enemy priority, full same-bearing names, quiet healthy state and disappearance of duplicate navigation when clearly visible. Knock out one and check brief neutral Out, no location/rescue urgency and persistent summary Out. Resize to ordinary and minimum windows. Check keyboard/controller button focus, text, countdown, pause, history scrolling, no overlaps, and readability at sixteen enemies. Assess animation, camera and frame smoothness. The source review and brief smoke cannot establish these full acceptance results.

No unit tests were added or run. Companion teams, upgrades, bosses, coins, multiplayer, infinite terrain, extra arenas and browser parity remain outside this prototype.

## Suggested next improvements

These are recommendations, not implemented gameplay requirements:

- An optional mid-run choice of launcher upgrades, after balancing short and long durations separately.
- A final-wave warning and a small results summary of wildlife saved, to make the closing moments easier to understand.
