Your first companion

Bring an agent
into the neighborhood.

Keep using your coding tool as usual. Pet Town follows its activity, so you can see when your companion is working, needs your input, or has finished.

  1. 01 / Move in

    Install the Mac app.

    Open the downloaded DMG, drag Pet Town into Applications, and launch it.

    Choose your Mac download →
  2. 02 / Connect

    Choose your coding tool.

    In Settings → Agents, open your tool’s card, choose Connect…, and review the setup. Herdr sessions are discovered automatically.

    Agent setup guide →
  3. 03 / Meet your companion

    Start a new session.

    Restart your coding tool or begin a new session after connecting. See your pet in Pet Street, then choose Open Pet Town to visit the 3D world.

    Using Codex? Open /hooks and verify the Pet Town hook first.

You can explore Pet Town before connecting an agent. Mayor voice is optional. Your coding tool’s usual account and usage charges still apply.
