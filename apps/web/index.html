move in.

Do I need Herdr?

No. Pet Village works as a standalone macOS app. Herdr adds exact agent-pane focus and built-in session awareness.

What stays on my Mac?

Your preferences, character packs, and monitoring state stay local. Pet Studio only contacts an image provider when you explicitly generate artwork.

What should I know about voice orchestration?

It is optional and off by default. Wake detection runs on-device, while spoken conversations use the services you configure.

Can I make the village less distracting?

Yes. Adjust motion, size, opacity, labels, and which pets join the cast. Reduced Motion is respected.

Which platforms does Pet Village support?

Pet Village currently ships for macOS on Apple Silicon and Intel. Windows and Linux downloads are not available yet.
