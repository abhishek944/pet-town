# Battle art

The four rigged skeletons reuse the archived KayKit Skeleton Minion, Warrior, Rogue and Mage assets. Their original CC0 notice is preserved in License.txt. The seven existing town rig clips were retained; Attack_C (overhead slam), Hit and Defeat were authored for the approved battle review. Additional attack variants in the exported GLBs are unused review variants.

The seed launcher was authored in Blender for Pet Town. Its editable .blend and authoring source, exact visual selections and animation resources remain under var/battle-mode/ui-ux-grill-me/2026-10-09-visuals/. Production has no runtime dependency on those ignored files, Blender, browser previews or image generation. Imported texture files beside the GLBs belong to these same licensed models.

All four skeleton appearances share one melee behavior and stats. The Mage model does not cast spells. Maple's existing source asset and locomotion clips remain unchanged.
