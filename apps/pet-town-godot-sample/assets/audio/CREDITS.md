# Island ambience

These four loops come from [Tidewater](https://github.com/dgreenheck/tidewater),
commit `4811ba48d795197de5621985f404e765c0b7c0ef`. Tidewater prepared the
recordings as seamless loops and published the source credits under CC0 1.0.
Pet Town converted Tidewater's Ogg Opus files to Ogg Vorbis for Godot; no new
recordings were added.

| File | Recording | Creator | License |
|---|---|---|---|
| `surf_far.ogg` | [Distant beach waves](https://freesound.org/people/chris_dagorne/sounds/426074/) | chris_dagorne | CC0 1.0 |
| `wind.ogg` | [Coastal path wind](https://freesound.org/people/bruno.auzet/sounds/706471/) | bruno.auzet | CC0 1.0 |
| `palms.ogg` | [Wind in palm trees](https://freesound.org/people/felix.blume/sounds/384550/) | felix.blume | CC0 1.0 |
| `birds_dawn.ogg` | [Hawaii birds](https://freesound.org/people/SorenF109/sounds/678859/) | SorenF109 | CC0 1.0 |
| `footsteps/grass_1.ogg` through `grass_5.ogg` | [Grass footsteps](https://freesound.org/people/SilentStrikeZ/sounds/389625/) | SilentStrikeZ | CC0 1.0 |

The five footstep clips were cut from Tidewater's `step_grass.ogg` sprite and
converted to Ogg Vorbis. They play only while the user drives a walking
companion.
