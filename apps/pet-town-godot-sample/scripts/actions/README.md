# Native land activities

`region_actions.setup(sample)` owns the exported library and all fifteen Sunmeadow, Willowmere, and Shellhaven destinations. Initialize `sample.ocean` first so the land journal can merge `ocean.journal_data()` and route `ocean.perform(id)`. `set_following(bool)` prevents explorer travel and land activities during companion control. `reset()` belongs in the confirmed world-reset flow.

The HUD contract is `set_journal_data(places, experiences, collection)` and `set_journal_reminder(entry)`. Cards use `title`, `art`, `meta`, `note`, and `actions: [{id, label, enabled}]`. Places retain the original IDs, names, coordinates and notes. The journal refreshes every 0.3 seconds, or 0.15 seconds during a fishing nibble. All pages retain the fishing reminder. The journal never grants an experience merely because its button was clicked.

`source-data.json` exports the original fifteen places, twenty-six catalog definitions and six coastal shell definitions. Models in the library are the original exported GLBs. Placement validates the complete dry, level footprint and native collider clearance before saving. Nearby original assets and personal additions can be replaced in place. Replacements use stable original placement keys, preserve hidden original geometry, and have Restore original controls. A shared thirty-change history undoes additions, replacements and restores. The library keeps the source 128-object limit. Unsupported saved placements suspend without erasing records and reappear when their ground is restored. Malformed asset saves remain untouched and block writes.

Land controllers reconstruct the original authored activity geometry at its fixed source coordinates. The six-flower planter has the original seeds → seedlings → blooms progression. Fishing waits 3.2–4 seconds and offers a four-second nibble window; catches save before being shown and gently release. Closing Journal, changing panels, following a companion, or losing application focus cancels fishing. Willowmere has the three original fish species and up to eight Kindness/Courage/Calm lanterns. The six shells retain their original names, colors, shapes and hints. Collection requires standing nearby on supported ground; featuring requires the supported Shell Cove shelf. The shelf has native solid collision and the saved favorite sparkles. All activity models suspend when edited terrain removes their complete dry, level support.

The native version-one `user://region-journal.json` record extends the existing stamp record with garden, fish/catches, wishes, shells and favorite. Existing native stamps remain valid. Native records use the isolated Godot user directory; original Three.js saves are not changed. Every award writes a temporary file and renames it before applying progress. The version-one asset record also adds a replacements array without invalidating existing native additions. Failed reads keep the original file and disable progress writes. Failed writes preserve the previous state.

## Review

Open Journal and visit each district. At Picnic Garden walk to the bed, plant and water twice. At Willowmere Lake cast, wait, reel during the nibble, switch to Collection while casting, and release the fish. At Lantern Grove hang all wish choices. Follow each coastal shell hint, collect nearby, then feature one beside the cove shelf. Reopen the application and inspect saved growth and collections. Remove support blocks beneath the planter, shore, posts, a shell and the shelf; confirm the visuals/actions suspend and restore when dry level ground returns. Following a companion must require Leave before land travel. Headless parser/import checks do not establish this live interaction or visual acceptance.
